# AI-Driven Coin Analyzer

1. **Advanced Forecasting**
   * Scans historical data, real-time order books, and on-chain developments to estimate short- to medium-term price patterns.
   * Includes margin-of-error estimates for measured, data-backed approach in uncertain markets.
2. **Sentiment & On-Chain Analytics**
   * Gathers a wide array of social media signals, dApp user engagement, and blockchain transaction volumes to form a weighted sentiment index.
   * Correlates sentiment with technical metrics, exposing potential trading imbalances or hype-driven surges.
3. **DeFi-Centric Metrics**
   * Tracks yield rates, lockup durations, and liquidity metrics in various DeFi projects, linking them with your coin holdings.
   * Assesses cross-chain potential, spotlighting pools or farms that might offer superior returns.
