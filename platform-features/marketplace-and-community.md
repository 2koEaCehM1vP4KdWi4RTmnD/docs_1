# Marketplace & Community

1. **Searchable Agent Directory**
   * Browse a comprehensive listing of publicly shared AI Agents, complete with performance scores, usage tips, and real user feedback.
   * Quick-deploy the agent of your choice with minimal setup friction.
2. **Collective Revenue Structures**
   * Co-creation workflows enable participants to build advanced multi-layer agents and split the earnings.
   * Encourages synergy and knowledge sharing among the AI Zynera developer ecosystem.
3. **Community-Driven Oversight**
   * Platform-wide enhancements are proposed and decided through voting, ensuring a democratic approach.
   * Token stakeholders can steer resource allocation, plugin approval processes, and overall strategic direction.
