# AI-Driven Wallet Analyzer

1. **Comprehensive Dashboard**
   * Display wallet balances, growth trajectories, and top movements in real time.
   * Pinpoint suspicious transactions or potential arbitrage openings directly in the interface.
2. **Predictive Modeling**
   * AI processes historical transactions and upcoming events (e.g., token unlocks) to forecast potential gains or losses.
   * Customized parameters can be set for immediate notifications on critical changes.
3. **Risk Management**
   * Integrated contract checks and code reviews provide warnings regarding vulnerable or malicious tokens.
   * Suggests systematic reallocation or partial withdrawals to mitigate high-risk concentrations.
