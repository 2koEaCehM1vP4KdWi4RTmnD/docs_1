# Navigating the Wallet Analyzer

RhelinaAI’s **Wallet Analyzer** uncovers a full suite of metrics and insights pertaining to on-chain funds, risk exposure, and overall profitability:

1. **Transaction Timeline**
   * Review a sequential narrative of deposits, withdrawals, and token swaps.
   * Use filters to hone in on specific transactions or intervals and pinpoint anomalies.
2. **Risk & Return Indicators**
   * Measure daily, weekly, or monthly growth of various tokens and liquidity investments.
   * Discover potential yield-farming lapses or highlight impermanent loss projections to optimize DeFi strategies.
3. **Custom Alerts**
   * Define personalized notifications for large trades or suspicious inbound transactions.
   * Get real-time alerts via app notifications or email when a wallet event meets your specified conditions.
4. **Consolidated Overview**
   * A single, integrated interface merges **AI-informed predictions** with raw transactional data, delivering a balanced perspective on wallet health.
