# Creating Your First AI Agent

Central to BloxanterAI is the **AI Agent Builder**, a no-code development hub to automate trading and analytics:

1. **Opening the Builder**
   * In the dashboard, click **AI Agents** and select **Create New Agent** to begin.
   * Choose a template or start from scratch.
2. **Setting Logic Parameters**
   * **Triggers**: Define the conditions that prompt the agent (e.g., token price surpassing a target level).
   * **Decision Blocks**: Structure the agent’s response—such as “If 24-hour volume spikes by 40%, move a portion of liquidity into that pool.”
3. **Modular Blocks**
   * Pick from curated components covering **DEX integration**, **portfolio optimization**, **NFT scanning**, and **automated arbitrage**.
   * Enrich your agent’s insight by linking to specialized oracles and analytics feeds.
4. **Validation & Release**
   * Use a **paper-trading** mode to verify your agent’s reliability and refine potential flaws.
   * Once satisfied, deploy on mainnet or testnet and oversee its performance in real conditions.
