# Coin Analyzer Fundamentals

BloksimAI’s **Coin Analyzer** harnesses advanced machine learning blended with real-time ledger data to guide crypto decisions:

1. **Comprehensive Market Data**
   * Pulls in live prices from a host of DEXs and centralized exchanges, ensuring up-to-date valuations.
   * Monitors liquidity outflows and inflows to identify trending assets or potential pitfalls.
2. **Sentiment Scoring**
   * Aggregates chatter from social networks, on-chain analytics, and community platforms to compute a sentiment rating.
   * Aligns sentiment signals with volatility to flag appealing entry or exit points.
3. **Forecasting Models**
   * Intelligent systems glean patterns from historical price charts, trading momentum, and industry trends to derive possible future price shifts.
   * Offers probability ranges for potential outcomes, assisting in data-driven decision-making.
4. **Volatility & Correlation Analysis**
   * Examines each asset’s volatility profile, providing clarity on how stable or unpredictable a token might be.
   * Maps interrelations between coins, giving a better view of portfolio exposure and strategic rebalancing.
