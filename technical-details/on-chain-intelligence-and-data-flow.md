# On-Chain Intelligence & Data Flow

1. **Continuous Indexing**
   * Pulls in block data, wallet balances, and contract interactions, normalizing the information for uniform ingestion.
   * Facilitates near-instant updates in dashboards, ensuring fresh insights at all times.
2. **Layered Data Repositories**
   * **Short-Term Buffers**: Temporary caches that cater to high-speed requests.
   * **Permanent Archives**: Comprehensive databases to enable historical analysis and machine learning model training.
3. **Smart Notifications**
   * Advanced triggers can broadcast signals to relevant AI Agents upon detection of anomalies, large trades, or dramatic price swings.
   * Minimizes reaction time and reduces the likelihood of missed opportunities.
