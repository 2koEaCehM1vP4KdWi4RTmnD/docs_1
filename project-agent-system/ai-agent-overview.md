# AI Agent Overview

BlockrioAI Agents function as autonomous, machine-guided entities that adapt their strategies over time:

1. **Machine Learning Feedback Loops**
   * Incorporates performance data after each cycle, polishing heuristics for improved yield, risk management, or trading success.
   * Ensures advanced optimization through continuous learning mechanisms.
2. **Contextual Awareness**
   * Agents gather **off-chain indicators** (e.g., global finance updates) and **on-chain data** (e.g., liquidity pools, token volumes) for holistic decision-making.
   * Bolsters resilience in unstable market states, including black swan events or short-term spikes.
3. **Use Case Highlights**
   * **DeFi Yield Maximization**: Automate harvesting and compounding across multiple protocols.
   * **NFT Procurement**: Monitor minting activity and snipe undervalued NFT listings.
   * **Trading & Market Making**: Configure custom strategies for liquidity provision and price stabilization.
