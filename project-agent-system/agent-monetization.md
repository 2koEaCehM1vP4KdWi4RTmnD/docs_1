# Agent Monetization

Convert your skillfully designed AI Agents into **profitable** assets:

1. **Tokenized Agents**
   * Establish agent-focused tokens that represent a share in the bot’s performance or the right to benefit from generated revenue.
   * Provide liquidity pools for these tokens, enabling outsiders to invest based on your agent’s track record.
2. **Profit Distribution**
   * **Smart Contract Automation**: Instant, trustless payouts to token holders whenever the agent earns transaction fees or yield profits.
   * **Performance Fees**: Dynamically vary commissions in line with the agent’s results, incentivizing ongoing improvements.
3. **Subscription-Based Access**
   * Permit fellow users to subscribe to your agent’s signals, paying either a flat fee or a portion of realized gains.
   * Incorporate on-chain governance, letting token holders modify risk parameters or direct the agent’s evolution.
